# Marketing Analytics — Learning Roadmap

## 1. Career Objective

My objective is to transition into Marketing Analytics, Performance Analytics or Business Intelligence.

I want to develop the practical ability to collect, clean, analyse and interpret business data, and communicate findings through reports and dashboards.

**Target roles:**
- Marketing Data Analyst
- Performance Marketing Analyst
- Marketing Analyst
- Junior Data Analyst
- Business Intelligence Analyst

**Target salary:** ₹50,000–₹70,000 per month.

## 3. Learning Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Excel and Data Analysis

- Data cleaning and validation
- Excel formulas and functions
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Power Query
- Marketing performance metrics
- Project 01: Paid Advertising Performance Analytics

### Phase 2 — SQL

- Database fundamentals
- SELECT, WHERE and ORDER BY
- Aggregate functions and GROUP BY
- JOIN operations
- CASE WHEN
- Subqueries and CTEs
- Window functions
- Business problem-solving using SQL

### Phase 3 — Power BI

- Data import and transformation
- Data modelling and relationships
- DAX measures
- Interactive dashboards
- Business reporting
- Project dashboard development

### Phase 4 — Marketing and Customer Analytics

- Google Analytics 4 fundamentals
- Website traffic and conversion funnels
- Customer acquisition analysis
- Customer retention and cohorts
- Customer lifetime value
- Campaign measurement and attribution

### Phase 5 — Advanced Skills and Portfolio

- Python fundamentals
- Pandas and NumPy
- Data analysis and visualisation
- Integrated business intelligence project
- Portfolio documentation
- Interview preparation

## 5. Study Commitment

**Planned study time:** 6 hours per day.

The learning process will prioritise practical assignments, independent problem-solving and demonstrable project outcomes over passive course completion.

## 6. Progress Measurement

Progress will be evaluated through:

- Ability to work independently with datasets.
- Accuracy of calculations and analysis.
- Ability to write SQL queries.
- Ability to build useful Power BI dashboards.
- Ability to explain findings in business language.
- Completion and documentation of portfolio projects.

## 7. Guiding Principle

The objective is not simply to learn tools or collect certificates.

The objective is to become capable of solving real business problems using data and to demonstrate that capability through genuine, documented work.
